About Me

Platforms Engineer & Cloud Infrastructure Specialist

I'm a Platforms Engineer passionate about building robust, scalable cloud-native platforms that enable development teams to ship faster and more reliably. I design and implement internal developer platforms that abstract away infrastructure complexity.

My expertise spans Kubernetes orchestration, infrastructure as code, CI/CD platform development, service mesh implementation, and cloud-native tooling. I focus on creating self-service platforms that improve developer productivity and operational excellence.

I work with cloud providers (AWS, GCP, Azure), container orchestration platforms, observability tools, and automation frameworks to build platforms that are reliable, secure, and developer-friendly.
